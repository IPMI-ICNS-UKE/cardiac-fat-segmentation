# cardiac-fat-segmentation
Tools for segmenting cardiac fat compartments, including epicardial adipose tissue
(EAT) and paracardial adipose tissue (PAT), in cardiac MRI, developed as
part of the project: *"Epicardial and Paracardial Adipose Tissue as Imaging Biomarkers of
Myocardial Scar and Outcome Predictors in the Hamburg City Health Study."*

## Contents

- [`SlicerModules/`](./SlicerModules) — a 3D Slicer extension for
  manual segmentation of the epicardium and pericardium in
  end-diastolic and end-systolic phases of 4D SAX cine MRI. Used to
  produce expert annotations for training and evaluation.
- *(planned)* segmentation model and inference scripts
- *(planned)* evaluation utilities
